Frequently asked questions

The essentials on access, cost, and what the three days involve.

The retreat is invitation-only, for CEOs, founders, managing directors, strategy leaders, and senior professionals involved in strategic decision-making. The community grows through referrals.

Two nights and three days of accommodation in a single or double room, full board (breakfast, lunch, and dinner; beverages not included), and the full programme of sessions and activities.

Rates are shared on request, with special rates for groups of more than three participants. Installment plans are available.

Matarma Bay, Ras Sudr, on the Gulf of Suez in South Sinai — roughly a two-hour drive from Cairo. Travel details are shared ahead of the retreat.
